Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “The eventual extremal inhibitory degree for simultaneous single-high multistability.”

The paper studies a concrete extremal question for signed monotone Boolean networks posed in Section 6.3 of Harshavardhan et al. (2026). It proves that the unique maximizing inhibitory degree is floor(n/2)-1 for all sufficiently large n. The key step is a localized polymer perturbation that resolves an exponentially small even-dimensional tie; the odd-dimensional candidates have leading weights 1:3:2.

The manuscript uses the published quantitative antichain results of Jenssen, Malekshahian and Park. It also gives an explicit conditional finite-dimensional certificate, carefully separated from the unconditional asymptotic theorem. It does not claim an unconditional numerical cutoff or a biological interpretation beyond the defined counting model.

Portable coefficient and threshold-audit scripts accompany the proofs.
